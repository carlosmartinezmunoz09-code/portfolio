A bit about me

I'm going into aerospace engineering, drawn mostly to what happens at high speed — the fluid dynamics and structural trade-offs that get genuinely hard once you push past Mach 1.

Most of what I do sits between making and simulating. I prototype through 3D printing, then check assumptions through real lfie testing. The gap between a sketch and a part you can hold is where I learn the most.

Outside of personal projects, TKS pushed my thinking on how to pitch and defend technical ideas, and the GreenPower team gave me real aero work under real manufacturing constraints.
